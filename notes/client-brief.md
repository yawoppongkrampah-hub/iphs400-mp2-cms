ASA is a Kenyon student group primarily for African students and students with African backgrounds, while welcoming anyone interested in African cultures and community. The website should make it easy for students to find upcoming events, including an Afrobeat event, learn about the association, explore cultural resources, and see photos from past activities. Event announcements should be quick to publish, while information about ASA and its resources should remain easy to find in the navigation. An ASA leader would manage accounts and permanent pages; another member could write and update event posts. The site should feel welcoming to someone discovering ASA for the first time. Photos should only be published when ASA has permission to use them.
